Two Populations

a game for two

You and a partner each control one population of neurons: your finger sets its activity, from silent (bottom) to fully active (top). Each of you gets a secret rule for reacting to the other. Play for 40 seconds (the game kicks you to new places every 8 seconds), then see which dynamics you created and compare them with the Wilson–Cowan equations for two populations.

Both of you enter the same class code and pair number.

Your pair as Wilson–Cowan equations

x is your activity, y your partner's. Each population moves towards S(…), an S-shaped response from 0 to 1 to the other population's activity, seen with a delay. The sign comes from your cards: + (Copycat) means the partner excites you, − (Contrarian) means it inhibits you. Nothing is fitted: the equations only take your reaction times from the round. Take the delays away and the copy–oppose pair stops oscillating.
